Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our products, colour mixing, colour guidance, availability and quotations.

01 What products does The Colour Alchemy offer?

We offer a wide range of paints, professional colour-mixing solutions, paint accessories, tools, handmade sinks, and stainless-steel taps.

02 Which paint brands are available?

We offer products from Dulux, JAT, Robbialac, and Causeway , subject to current availability.

03 Do you provide professional colour mixing?

Yes. Our Colour Lab provides professional colour mixing to help you achieve the desired colour for your interior or exterior project.

04 Can you help me choose the right paint and colour?

Absolutely. Our team can guide you in selecting suitable colours and paint products based on your space, surface, and project requirements.

05 How can I get a quotation or check product availability?

You can contact us by phone or WhatsApp with your requirements. Our team will assist you with product availability, colour-mixing enquiries, and quotation requests.
