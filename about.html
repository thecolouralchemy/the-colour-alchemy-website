Saheed Sons

The business behind The Colour Alchemy

The Colour Alchemy is powered by Saheed Sons and is based at 53 Old Tangalle Road, Kotuwegoda, Matara. The showroom focuses on paint, colour and practical project support.

The Colour Alchemy concept brings that focus together around professional colour mixing, colour selection, paint products, accessories and tools, with handmade sinks and stainless-steel taps available as supporting ranges.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the project, help narrow the product and colour options, support the mixing process where appropriate, and make it easy to contact the team for current availability.
